## Scope
Home one-pager (index.html) plus realisaties.html. Mode: Persuade. Audience: Belgian homeowners and builders planning a bathroom, heating or wellness job, often on a phone. Action: request an offerte or call 09 253 20 37. Proof: their own ~50 project photos, owners on site, Cerga/G1-G2, erkend Hansgrohe/Hansa installateur. No invented reviews, numbers or address details.

## Direction contract
THESIS: One aanspreekpunt that connects every trade, shown as one continuous brand-blue installation line that runs through the whole page and branches into each service, the projects and the offerte. Refuses the installer default of a stock bathroom hero over three icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, brand blue #0080BC as the only colour (line, primary action, one full blue field at the close), cool steel grey for secondary text, deep blue ink for type. Barlow Condensed (echoing the logo lettering) for display, Barlow for text. Pipe language: 2px blue stroke, rounded 90-degree bends, small ring fittings at junctions. Square photo corners, pill-free, buttons with 2px radius.
STORY: Visitor sees who they are and what they do in one glance, follows the line through sanitair, verwarming, wellness and the three-step way of working, browses real projects (marking ones they like), and lands in a short offerte form that already carries their marked projects. Phone always one tap away.

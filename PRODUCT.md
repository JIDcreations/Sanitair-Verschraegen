## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user choice). Must host on plain shared hosting.

## Users

Homeowners and professional builders (aannemers, architects, horeca owners) in Belgium planning a new build or renovation: a new bathroom, central heating, a heat pump, or a wellness room. They arrive comparing installers, want to see real finished work, judge whether this firm is reliable, and then call or request a quote. Many visit on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Website for Sanitair Verschraegen, an installation company for sanitair, verwarming (heating), ventilatie and wellness, founded 2004. The site must make a visitor trust the firm enough to request an offerte (quote) or call. Success: more quote requests and calls, and a site the owners are proud to send people to. This is a redesign pitch to a potential client; it must impress while staying easy to use.

## Positioning

One aanspreekpunt (single point of contact) from bouwwerf to luxury bathroom: the firm coordinates all trades itself (breaking-out, carpentry, masonry, tiling, planning, pipes and fixtures), and the owners Mario and Ann Verschraegen work on site themselves. Registered subcontractors only work with their own boss present on site. Recognised Cerga installer, G1/G2 heating, Eandis partner, recognised Hansgrohe and Hansa installer.

## Operating Context

- Clients first visit a showroom partner (Toch, Facq, Caluwaerts) to choose, then Verschraegen visits on site to check feasibility.
- Mandatory two-yearly maintenance of gas appliances, with certificates for tax deduction.
- Works for private clients and professionals, including many horeca businesses; clients across all of Belgium.

## Product Principles

1. Real work is the proof: let their own projects carry the persuasion.

3. Say it short: a visitor should know what they do within seconds.
